Information

WoodCase is a handmade furniture storefront designed by Abhinandan. The brief was a quiet brand site for collections and pieces — not a checkout — published at abhiiiijain.github.io/WOODCASE.

About WoodCase

We build from reclaimed teak in India and show the work as a catalog you can browse. If a piece is right for a room, enquire and we will confirm finish, size, and a making date.

Installation Service

Our team can assemble and place your furniture in the room it was made for. Book installation when you order, or add it later through customer service.

Delivery Service

We deliver across India from our workshop. Lead times depend on the piece and your city; we confirm dates before the order is finished.

Measuring Service

If you want a piece fitted to a wall or alcove, we can visit, measure, and advise on the right size from the collection.

Customer Service

Questions about an order, a finish, or a repair go to the same desk. Write to us through the contact details below and we will reply within two working days.

Return Policy

Unused items can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Custom or made-to-measure pieces are excluded. We arrange collection once the return is approved.

Contact Us

Email hello@woodcase.example or visit the store listed under Stores. Include your order number if you have one.

Gift Guide

Smaller shelves and stools from Shelfmate and Caterpillar work as gifts. We can hold a piece for a date you choose.

Feedback

If something about the site or a product is unclear, tell us. We use those notes when we update the collection pages.

FAQ

Most pieces are solid reclaimed teak with iron or woven details. Care is a dry cloth and, once or twice a year, a light oil on the wood. Lead time is usually four to six weeks.

Stores

Visit the WoodCase showroom in India to see finishes in person. Hours and the current address are confirmed by email before you travel.

Newsroom

Press requests and collection notes can be sent to the contact address. We will share images and finish samples where we can.

Working at WoodCase

Workshop and studio roles open with each collection. Write to us with a short note and examples of your work.

Sustainability

We build from certified reclaimed teak taken from old houses, and we keep metalwork local to the workshop. Offcuts are used for samples and smaller pieces.

WoodCase for Business

Hotels, offices, and retailers can order in volume or ask for a finish match. Start with the contact address and the rooms you need to furnish.
